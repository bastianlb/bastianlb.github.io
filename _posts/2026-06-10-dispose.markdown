We introduce <emph>DisPOSE</emph>, a self-supervised framework that approximates the discrete multi-view person-assignment problem as a generative diffusion process over polystochastic tensors, using differentiable Sinkhorn projections to guide solutions toward valid assignments from 2D image priors — retaining 99% of its performance with only 10% of the pseudo-labels and remaining nearly agnostic to camera arrangement.
